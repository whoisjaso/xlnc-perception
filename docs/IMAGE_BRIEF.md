# Image brief

The site renders a typographic plate wherever a photograph is missing, so nothing is broken today. Drop files at the paths below and they appear immediately. JPEG, sRGB, quality 80 to 85.

## Vehicles (`public/media/vehicles/<slug>.jpg`)

One hero per car. 2400 × 1500 (16:10). Dark studio or night exterior, three-quarter front view, car occupying about 70% of the frame, lower third clear enough for text to sit over it, no visible plates.

Prompt template for a generator:

> Editorial automotive photograph of a {year} {make} {model} in {color}, three-quarter front view, parked on wet dark asphalt at night under a single soft overhead light, deep charcoal background fading to black, subtle reflections, no people, no license plate, no text, 35mm lens, f/4, cinematic, high detail, 16:10.

Real photography of the actual cars beats generated images. Shoot at dusk on the lot with one light and the same angle for every car so the fleet grid reads as a set.
